# ADX: The Open Full-Stack Standard for Enclosure-Native Modular Electronics
## Build the board. Snap in the box. Deploy in the field.

ADX is a 3-tier open standard providing complete design references across mechanical enclosures, industrial field hardware, and driverless web tooling:

1. Mechanical Reference (8748 Form Factor): 87 x 48 mm enclosure-native PCB, 10 mm M3 keep-outs, 3 mm rail margins, single-edge I/O, and 3D CAD models.
2. Electrical Reference (Bus & Field Network): Industrial −40°C~+105°C BOM, isolated RS-485 (LN-485), and 20-pin IDC modular bus (5 V/2 A & 3.3 V logic).
3. Application Reference (Web MIDI & Tooling): Web MIDI intelligent modem (RPR4), driverless browser PWAs, and open SDK/templates for specialized field web apps.
