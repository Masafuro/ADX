# ADX: The Open Hardware Standard for Enclosure-Native Modular Electronics
## Build the board. Snap in the box. Deploy in the field.

ADX is a 3-tier open hardware standard designed to eliminate enclosure headaches, multi-side connector cutouts, and M3 screw hazards:

1. 8748 Form Factor: 87 x 48 mm PCB, 10 mm M3 keep-outs, 3 mm rail margins, and 67 mm single-edge I/O.
2. ADX 20-Pin Bus: 5 V/2 A power & 3.3 V logic (UART/I2C/SPI/GPIO) via 2x10 IDC.
3. Ecosystem: MCU COREs, Power CARDs, enclosure 3D/CAD models, WebSerial tools, and more.
