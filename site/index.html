Flagship Standard & Ecosystem

ADX (Advanced Devices eXtended)

Prototyping on a workbench with jumper wires is where great ideas start. But deploying them inside factory control cabinets, outdoor fixtures, or custom enclosures requires a different kind of hardware. ADX is our physical and electrical answer: "The Working Arduino."

01 / FORM FACTOR

8748 Physical Profile

Standardized 87.0 × 48.0 mm footprint. Generous Φ5.0 mm washer clearances for real M3 screws, plus edge grab margins for slide-in rails and 3D-printed snap cases.

02 / WIRING HARNESS

Unidirectional Edge

All external power and field communication lines strictly aligned along a single PCB edge. Say goodbye to four-sided wire routing and multi-cutout enclosure panels.

03 / MODULAR BUS

Universal 20-Pin Bus

Gravity fail-safe high-current power routing, co-planar analog shielding, and 3.3V native logic interconnecting Core controllers and Card expansions.

Modular Architecture

One Ecosystem, Infinite Configurations

Designed around single-responsibility modules: Core controllers do compute; Cards handle power and physical isolation.

CORE CONTROLLER

ADX CORE-U

Standard USB development core. Dual Type-C ports (5V/2A power + dual USB-UART), ATtiny1616 MCU, and case-friendly flashing with zero button requirements.

INDUSTRIAL CONTROLLER

ADX CORE-A

Harsh environment controller. -40°C to +105°C industrial rating, 2500V galvanic isolated RS-485, and tool-free push-in spring clamp terminals for rock-solid field wiring.

EXPANSION CARDS

CARD Ecosystem

Modular expansion cards including isolated DC 12V/24V power supplies, AAA battery autonomy cards, and pass-through prototyping boards.

Engineering Heritage

Backed by Furukawa Kikaku (古川企画)

ARLE.cc is an independent global hardware brand founded by Furukawa Kikaku, an embedded engineering firm based in Sasebo, Nagasaki, Japan.

With years of experience delivering robust firmware, custom electronics, and industrial control systems across Japan, our philosophy is simple: engineering integrity above all. We build open-hardware tools that honor physical laws, respect engineering constraints, and run reliably in the real world.
