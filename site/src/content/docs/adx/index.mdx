**ADX (Advanced Devices eXtended)** is an enclosure-native open-hardware modular ecosystem developed by **ARLE.cc**. Its goal is grounded and practical: **"The Working Arduino"** — making it straightforward to take sketches that work on a workbench and mount, wire, and operate them reliably inside real enclosures.

<Aside type="note" title="Portal Rollout Status (M1)">
  This documentation portal is being rolled out incrementally alongside physical prototype boards. Authoritative specifications, schematics, and Gerbers are always available in the <a href="https://github.com/Masafuro/ADX">Masafuro/ADX GitHub repository</a>.
</Aside>

## Core Physical & Electrical Foundation

Every ADX Core board and expansion CARD shares two open standards:

| Layer | Active Standard | Key Highlights |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mechanical Profile** | **8858 Form Factor** (`v0.1-alpha`) | `88.0 × 58.0 mm` C0 profile, `76.0 × 46.0 mm` M3 pitch with `Φ10.0 mm` washer keep-out, `3.0 mm` card guides, single-edge `66.0 mm` I/O Edge, `M1–M4` chiral optical markers |
| **Interconnect Bus** | **ADX 20-Pin Bus** (`ADX-BUS-SPEC-v4.2`) | 2×10P 2.54 mm shrouded IDC header, logical pin IDs (`S00`, `P01`–`P10`), panel-aligned bottom `VBUS` (`4.5–5.0V`, `2A–3A`), Pin 16 `GND` boundary wall, native `3.3V` LVCMOS I/O |
