## ADX 20-Pin Bus Quick Reference (`ADX-BUS-SPEC-v4.2`)

- **Logical Pin IDs**: `S00` (System `OWD / PROG`, Card N.C. required) and `P01`–`P10` (user/Card signals, `3.3V` LVCMOS).
- **Power Bus (`VBUS`, Pins 17–20)**: `4.5V – 5.0V DC`, `2.0A – 3.0A` continuous, bottom-aligned `2×2` copper cluster with mandatory reverse-current & over-current protection on sourcing modules.
- **Boundary & Shielding**: Pin 16 `GND` protective wall above `VBUS`; `P01` (`AIN`) shielded between Pin 1 and Pin 4 `GND`; `P02` (`EXTCLK`) guarded by Pin 4 and Pin 6 `GND`; Pin 11 `GND` separates I2C (`P05–P06`) from SPI (`P07–P10`).
- **Firmware Macros (`ADX_<BOARD>.h`)**: Always use the collision-safe `ADX_P01`–`ADX_P10` prefix (never bare `P01`–`P10`).
