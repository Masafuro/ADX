## ADX 20-Pin Bus Quick Reference (`ADX-BUS-SPEC-v5.0`)

- **Logical Pin IDs**: Pure sequential **`P01`–`P11`** (all 11 user/Card signals unrestricted, `3.3V` LVCMOS). Legacy `S00` (`OWD`/`UPDI`) is omitted from the IDC bus and handled internally by each Core board for HV-noise immunity.
- **3-Wire Field UART (`P03`–`P05`, Pins 6–8)**: Dedicated RS-485 Driver Enable **`P03` (`UART_DE` / `XDIR`)** on Pin 6 (mandatory `10kΩ–100kΩ` Card-side pull-down), `P04` (`UART_TX`), and `P05` (`UART_RX`).
- **Power Bus (`VBUS`, Pins 17–20)**: `4.5V – 5.25V DC`, `2.0A – 3.0A` continuous bidirectional power, bottom-aligned `2×2` copper cluster with mandatory reverse-current & over-current protection on sourcing modules.
- **Boundary & Shielding**: Pin 16 `GND` protective wall above `VBUS`; triple odd-row ground guard (`Pins 1, 3, 5 GND`) coplanar-shielding `P01` (`AIN`, Pin 2) and `P02` (`EXTCLK`, Pin 4); Pin 11 `GND` separates I2C (`P06–P07`) from SPI (`P08–P11`).
- **Firmware Macros (`ADX_<BOARD>.h`)**: Always use the collision-safe `ADX_P01`–`ADX_P11` prefix and aliases (`ADX_AIN`, `ADX_CLK`, `ADX_DE`, `ADX_TX`, `ADX_RX`, `ADX_SCL`, `ADX_SDA`, `ADX_CS`, `ADX_MOSI`, `ADX_MISO`, `ADX_SCK`) — never bare `P01`–`P11`.
