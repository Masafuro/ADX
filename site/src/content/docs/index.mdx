## Built Around Three Perspectives

ADX is designed from day one so hardware boards, enclosures, and expansion circuits share one clean physical and electrical contract.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Hardware Catalog" icon="laptop">
    Board-by-board visual pinouts, specifications, and fabrication data for **ADX CORE-U**, **Reference CARD v0**, and **Prototype CARD v0** *(rolling out as physical boards arrive)*.
  </Card>
  <Card title="For Enclosure Designers" icon="setting">
    The **8858 Form Factor (`88.0 × 58.0 mm`)** with `Φ10.0 mm` M3 washer clearances, `3.0 mm` card guides, single-edge `66.0 mm` I/O alignment, and `M1–M4` optical alignment markers.
  </Card>
  <Card title="For Circuit & CARD Designers" icon="puzzle">
    The **ADX 20-Pin Bus (`v4.2`)** featuring MCU-independent logical pin IDs (`S00`, `P01`–`P10`), bottom-aligned `VBUS` (`2A–3A`), coplanar analog shielding, and native `3.3V` LVCMOS signaling.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Open Standards & SSOT" icon="open-book">
    Specifications are maintained in English as a Single Source of Truth in GitHub and surfaced here alongside Japanese guides and CUD-compliant visual reference sheets.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
