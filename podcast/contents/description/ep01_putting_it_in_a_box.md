## Description — YouTube / Show Notes 拡張版（文字数制限が緩い媒体向け）

```text
We live in a world with the Arduino UNO and the $4 Raspberry Pi Pico—so why on earth spend months designing custom microcontroller boards from scratch?

Because boards designed to be heroes on a breadboard start fighting you the moment you put them inside an enclosure:
- Missing mounting holes (hello, hot glue)
- Loose Dupont jumper wires hidden behind a closed lid
- BOOTSEL and RESET buttons locked inside a sealed box
- Trapped heat softening PLA and throwing off onboard sensors

Putting a board in a box isn't just packaging—it's the moment a hobby experiment has to become a human tool. We still don't have all the answers, and that ongoing struggle is the origin story of ADX.
```
